# KEEPER
Your personal PC and gaming tracker and encrypted account vault.

Vault accounts can be edited, and the vault can lock automatically after a configurable idle period. On Windows, KEEPER can start minimized to the system tray; closing the window keeps it running there until you choose Quit from the tray menu.

Choose Glossy or Normal in Settings to switch the surface treatment across the app. KEEPER uses its bundled DM Sans and Space Grotesk fonts and the KEEPER shield artwork throughout the interface.
